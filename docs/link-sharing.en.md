# URL and text sharing

[简体中文](link-sharing.md) · English

anDrop does not support sharing URLs or text directly in either direction. It does not offer Send Link, Copy Link, Open Link or an automatic-open setting. The macOS Share extension accepts files, images, movies and complete Live Photo bundles.

Existing `.url`, `.webloc` and `.txt` files can still be transferred as ordinary files. They are saved in the selected receive folder without interpreting their contents, opening links or automatically removing the received file.

OPPO's Apple-oriented sharing route rejects a pure URL/text item before transmission. Native Android discovery, connection and text transport were investigated but are not included in the product.
