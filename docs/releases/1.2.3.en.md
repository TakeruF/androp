# anDrop 1.2.3

[简体中文](https://github.com/TakeruF/androp/blob/main/docs/releases/1.2.3.md) · English

## Automatic notification cleanup

- Sent, received and failed transfer notifications now disappear from Notification Center three minutes after delivery. Previously, automatic cleanup covered only successful sends, leaving received and failed notifications behind.
- Each notification keeps its own deadline. New notifications do not postpone cleanup, and cleanup continues while a menu is open.
- Overdue notifications, including those from older versions, are cleared when anDrop launches or the Mac wakes from sleep. While anDrop is quit, cleanup waits until the next launch.
- Transfer history and diagnostic logs remain available in the app. Approval requests and active-transfer notifications retain their actions.

## Update and installation

Check for updates in anDrop Settings, or download **anDrop-1.2.3-arm64.dmg** and replace anDrop in Applications. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Existing settings and trusted-device records are retained.
