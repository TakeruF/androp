# anDrop 1.2.0

[简体中文](https://github.com/TakeruF/androp/blob/main/docs/releases/1.2.0.md) · English

**Official Huawei Share support is the headline feature.** Use the phone's built-in sharing for bidirectional Mac transfers, without a phone app, account, USB or 鸿蒙星河互联. Both devices need Bluetooth and the same Wi-Fi/LAN.

## Huawei Share

- Dedicated discovery, sending/receiving, approval for every receipt, progress/history, selected-folder saving and collision-safe names.
- Enroll Huawei from completed receipts using its transfer public key and proof of private-key possession. Receive once in Everyone and manually add it; every transfer still requires approval. This is not Huawei account pairing.
- Improved photo-thumbnail/preview compatibility. Huawei→Mac: at most 16 files,16 MiB each and total.
- Mac→Huawei:16 files,4 GiB each/16 GiB total, disk snapshots/chunked encryption. Local transport validated; GiB-scale physical transfers unverified.
- Pura70/HarmonyOS7 bidirectional contents validated; other Huawei coverage pending.

## Sending and device management

Click a device to send immediately while other candidates stay clickable. Send the same batch to different devices with independent progress/results/cancel/retry. Concurrent integrity passed simulated peers and real loopback HTTPS/WebSocket; simultaneous physical Huawei/OPPO delivery remains unverified. Receiving remains exclusive.

Icon context menus rename/hide; Settings restores hidden devices and persists state through restart. Names are Mac-local. Huawei artwork/computer badges do not establish every brand/PC's compatibility. Extension-derived image/video/audio declarations improve receiving prompts.

## Updates and 鸿蒙星河互联

Verified updates install/relaunch automatically when idle and notify, deferred during transfers. With auto-install off, notifications announce availability. Finish 鸿蒙星河互联 transfers and quit it before using independent anDrop Huawei sharing. Coexistence is experimental; failure while 鸿蒙星河互联 runs queues a recommendation without quitting another app or proving it caused the failure.

## Installation and limitations

Open anDrop-1.2.0-arm64.dmg, replace Applications/anDrop, or Check for Updates. Apple Silicon/macOS 14+, no Intel. macOS 14 packaging checks passed, physical14 remains untested. App Developer ID signed/notarized/stapled, DMG Developer ID signed, updates additionally verified. Compress folders and split same-named files across batches. Quick Share/Wi-Fi Direct absent. Trusted-only filters requests, not visibility, with approval always required.

Updated production-view screenshots use fictional devices/files. See [validation](https://github.com/TakeruF/androp/blob/main/docs/testing.en.md) and [compatibility](https://github.com/TakeruF/androp/blob/main/docs/compatibility.en.md).
