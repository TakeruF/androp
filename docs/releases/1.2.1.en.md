# anDrop 1.2.1

[简体中文](https://github.com/TakeruF/androp/blob/main/docs/releases/1.2.1.md) · English

## Automatically accept from trusted devices

- Enrolled trusted devices are accepted automatically by default in both Everyone and Trusted Devices Only modes.
- Settings adds **Automatically accept from trusted devices**. Turn it off to approve every incoming transfer. Your choice persists across restarts.
- Applies to enrolled Alliance sender IDs and Huawei Share transfer public keys. New devices still require an approved receipt in Everyone mode, followed by manual enrollment.
- Untrusted devices always require approval in Everyone mode and are rejected in Trusted Devices Only mode. Receive scope does not hide this Mac from nearby devices.
- Automatic acceptance skips the approval prompt. Transfer start, completion and failure notifications remain, with progress and cancellation available in the menu bar.

Alliance sender IDs are self-reported; this convenience filter is not cryptographic identity authentication. Huawei Share checks possession of the enrolled key during each receive handshake. Changed sender IDs or keys require a new receipt and enrollment.

## Update and installation

Check for updates in anDrop Settings, or download **anDrop-1.2.1-arm64.dmg** and replace anDrop in Applications. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Automatic acceptance defaults to on when upgrading to this version and can be disabled at any time.

Retains Huawei Share, concurrent sending and device management from 1.2.0. Chinese/English documentation and Settings screenshots are updated. See the [test record](https://github.com/TakeruF/androp/blob/main/docs/testing.en.md) for validation scope and limitations.
