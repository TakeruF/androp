# anDrop 1.3.1

[简体中文](https://github.com/TakeruF/androp/blob/main/docs/releases/1.3.1.md) · English

## Receive reliability

- Fixed the Mac remaining busy briefly after a completed receive, causing an Android device's next send to fail.
- Initial connections now also retry unclassified socket failures that lack an error number. Recovery is limited to four attempts within ten seconds; established file transfers are never replayed.

## Validation and compatibility

- Confirmed four consecutive sends from an OPPO tablet to the Mac.
- All 305 Python tests passed, including regressions for immediate idle state during delayed connection cleanup, protection of the next receive, and bounded connection retries.
- Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later.

## Updating and installation

Check for updates in anDrop Settings, or download **anDrop-1.3.1-arm64.dmg** and replace anDrop in Applications. Existing settings, trusted devices and transfer history are retained.
